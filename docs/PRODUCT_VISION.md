# Product vision

## V6 future product direction (proposed, not delivered)

TaleAtlas intends to become a reader-centered social discovery platform, combining trustworthy manga/manhwa/manhua/novel metadata and private reading records with opt-in profiles, a moderated newsfeed, reader following, explainable recommendations, and performance-conscious motion. The immediate priority is V6A UI and hosted account reliability; V6B–V6F are separate gated proposals, not shipped features. Existing V3 reading data remains private, V4 public community posting remains disabled on staging pending hosted approval, and external providers remain disabled pending rights review. See [V6 plan](V6_PLAN.md), [V6A UI stabilization](V6A_UI_STABILIZATION.md), [social design](V6_SOCIAL_PRODUCT.md), [metadata automation](V6_METADATA_AUTOMATION.md), [recommendations and motion](V6_RECOMMENDATIONS_MOTION.md), and [validation ledger](V6_VALIDATION.md).

TaleAtlas helps readers discover stories and curate private reading records, with English and Vietnamese experiences. V0 implements account safeguards; V1 the curated catalog; V2 manual/offline requests, limited metadata correction and human review; V3 the private reading library, progress and rereads; V4 explicit public Work discussions/replies, spoiler-aware reveals, deliberately separate public reviews/ratings and manual moderation.
